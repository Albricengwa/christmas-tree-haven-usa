Our Christmas Tree Varieties

Each tree is cut fresh on-site and hand-inspected for fullness, shape, and fragrance.

Most Popular

Fraser Fir

Best needle retention & fragrance

The gold standard of Christmas trees. Symmetrical shape, rich emerald color, and that incredible Christmas scent that lasts all season.

  • Excellent needle retention
  • Strong, iconic fragrance
  • Dense, full branches
  • Holds heavy ornaments well
From$906–7 ft
Premium Pick

Noble Fir

Sturdy branches, long-lasting

Elegant and sophisticated. Blue-green needles, stiff branches that hold the heaviest ornaments, and a clean, long-lasting display.

  • Stiff, strong branches
  • Beautiful blue-green color
  • Very long-lasting
  • Less resin, cleaner indoors
From$956–7 ft

Balsam Fir

Classic aroma, great value

The traditional Christmas tree many families grew up with. Soft, dark green needles and one of the sweetest, most nostalgic pine fragrances.

  • Classic Christmas aroma
  • Soft, flexible needles
  • Rich dark green color
  • Great value
From$756–7 ft

Douglas Fir

Full & fragrant, great price

A reliably full, bushy tree with a sweet, mild citrus-pine scent. One of the most popular farm trees for good reason.

  • Naturally full, bushy shape
  • Sweet, mild fragrance
  • Soft needles
  • Budget-friendly
From$656–7 ft

Colorado Blue Spruce

Stunning color, symmetrical

A showstopper with its distinctive blue-gray foliage and naturally symmetrical shape. Perfect for a sophisticated holiday look.

  • Striking blue-gray color
  • Naturally symmetrical
  • Very stiff branches
  • Holds heavy decor
From$856–7 ft

Scotch Pine

Keeps needles, budget pick

A hardy, reliable tree with excellent needle retention — even when dry. Great traditional choice at a friendly price.

  • Best needle retention
  • Classic bright green
  • Strong branches
  • Most affordable
From$556–7 ft

Our Story

Growing Christmas Memories, One Tree at a Time

Christmas Tree Haven USA is a family-owned farm dedicated to bringing the joy of a real, fresh-cut Christmas tree to families in our community. We grow our trees with care, cut them fresh when you arrive, and make the whole experience part of your holiday tradition.

Whether it's your first tree or your fiftieth, we'll help you find the perfect one — and make the trip out to the farm something the whole family looks forward to every year.
