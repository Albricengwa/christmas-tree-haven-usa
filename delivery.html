How it works

From our hands to your front room.

Ordering is personal rather than transactional. You choose your tree, we confirm the details with you directly, and then we arrange delivery to your address.

  1. 1 · Choose your tree. Browse the collection and note the height and price that suits your room.
  2. 2 · Message us on Messenger. Send the tree name and your delivery address or ZIP code.
  3. 3 · We confirm availability and delivery. We will tell you what is possible for your location before you commit.
  4. 4 · Your tree is delivered. Decorated, packed and ready to put up.

Good to know

What we confirm before you order.

Delivery to your address

We deliver across the United States. Share your address or ZIP code on Messenger and we will confirm delivery to your area.

Delivery cost

Delivery charges depend on your destination and the tree you choose. We quote them for you directly before you commit — nothing is added later.

Timing

Timing varies by location and season. We agree a delivery window with you in Messenger rather than promising dates we cannot keep.

Large trees

Our 9 ft trees are substantial. Tell us about access — stairs, doorways, lifts — and we will advise on the best way to get it inside.

Before it arrives

A few minutes of preparation.

Leave room above the tree for the topper. A 7.5 ft tree needs roughly eight feet of clearance; a 9 ft tree needs closer to ten. If you are unsure, send us your ceiling height on Messenger and we will advise.

Clear the corner or alcove where the tree will stand, and check the route from your door — doorways, hallways and stairs. Our larger trees are heavy and wide once assembled.

Send your full delivery address or ZIP code with the tree you would like. That is all we need to confirm availability, cost and timing for your order.
