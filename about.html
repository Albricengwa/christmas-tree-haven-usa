About us

A Christmas tree house, built around one evening.

The moment the tree goes up, the season begins. Everything we sell is chosen for that moment.

Our story

Real trees, without the trade-offs.

Christmas Tree Haven USA began with a simple frustration: a real tree is beautiful for a fortnight and then it is a job — watering, dropping needles, sweeping up, taking it out. An artificial tree should feel like the best of both worlds, but most of them arrive bare.

So we built a collection the other way around. Every tree is styled first — ornaments, ribbon, lights, topper — and only then sold. You open the box, you put it up, and the room is finished. No shopping for baubles in December, no mismatched decorations, no compromise.

We are an American brand serving customers across the United States. We keep the range small and considered, and we speak to every customer directly through Facebook Messenger, so the person answering your question is the person who knows the trees.

What we stand for
